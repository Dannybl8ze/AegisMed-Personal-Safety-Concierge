# Dashboard Page Overrides

> **Project:** AegisMed caregiver dashboard (the only page in the app)
> Rules here override `../MASTER.md`. Anything not listed follows the master.

The master file was generated by the `ui-ux-pro-max` skill. It recommends the
"Accessible & Ethical" style (high contrast, 16px text, visible focus, 44px
targets, reduced motion). This page keeps those rules and takes its visual
language from the reference the owner chose, the "BioTrack Medical Dashboard"
concept by Fulcrum Rocks: a pale neutral page, white cards with large radii, a
slim icon rail, pill-shaped controls, a large light-weight display heading with
a row of figures, and pastel status pills. The master's "Page Pattern" section
describes a marketing landing page and does not apply here.

## What keeps it from looking generic

- One neutral palette. Colour appears only where it carries meaning (risk,
  held, approved, rejected, error), never as decoration.
- No gradients, glows, glass blur or emoji.
- Display type is Urbanist at light weight and tight leading; body is Figtree.
- Controls are charcoal or white pills. The single amber button is the one
  cautionary action, Approve Override.
- Every rail item, figure and button does something real; nothing is filler.

## Rules specific to this page

- **Status is never colour alone.** Risk shows as filled steps plus "High risk";
  decisions, held messages and errors carry an icon and a text label.
- **Targets are 44px.** Buttons use the `touch` size; inputs, the patient
  switcher and its options are 44px high.
- **Body text is 16px.** Secondary text is 14px; 12px is used only for message
  meta lines, table headers and the risk label.
- **Focus is always visible.** Controls show a 3px ring; other focusable
  elements get a 3px outline with a 2px offset.
- **Motion is optional.** Animations and smooth scrolling are disabled under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; the "located" highlight then shows as a
  static ring.
- **Icons are SVG** from `lucide-react`. Patient avatars are initials.
- **Shape:** cards 28px radius, inner surfaces 24px, controls fully rounded.
  One soft shadow (`shadow-card`) for everything that floats on the page.

## Layout

- From 1024px: icon rail on the left (brand mark, three section links, Reset
  at the bottom), a top bar (wordmark, patient switcher, Add Patient), then two
  columns: the conversation card and the review portal. The portal scrolls on
  its own.
- Below 1024px: the rail is hidden and Reset moves into the top bar; one
  column, conversation first, portal below. Nothing scrolls sideways at 375px.
- A skip link jumps to the medication request field.
